# 句式库

主手册 `SKILL.md` 定规矩。这里的模板**原样填空就发**，不等审批；改了问句、加了事实、顺着他的话写的，按"聊"处理（SKILL §4，大多数情况也可以直接发，发完报）。`[ ]` 里换成真实信息；没有就删掉那半句，不要硬凑。

---

## 首条

**A · 只留联系方式**（机器在留资 15 分钟后发，人不手发；供核对）
```
Hi! Rowling from Real Hibachi - our system should've texted you a price and didn't, sorry about that. For 15 adults it's $838.50 Fri-Sun, or $763.50 Mon-Thu (2 proteins each + fried rice, veggies, salad and the chef show). What date are you thinking?
```

**B · 报价已发**（机器在报价 2 分钟后发；供核对和手发时用）
```
Hi! Rowling from Real Hibachi 👋 [Sat, Oct 18] is open on our end for your [N] in [City] - what's the celebration?
Hi! Rowling from Real Hibachi 👋 Saw your quote for [N] - what's the celebration?      ← 没日期或没像样的城市名
```

**C · 客人先问**：≤2 句答 + 一个问题。
```
Hi, it's Rowling from Real Hibachi. We do! Temecula's inside our area - travel's usually $20-40 depending on the address. Roughly how many guests are you thinking?
```

**电话之后的书面确认**（挂断几分钟内）
```
Hi! Rowling from Real Hibachi - you just spoke with Bling, here's the link he promised. [Friday, Nov 13 at 7 PM in Downey]: [your 13 adults and two 7-year-olds] come to $[X][, and there's no travel fee]. Lock the date here - it just saves a card, nothing is charged today: <link>
```

**未接、秒挂的陌生号**（只发一条）
```
Hi! Rowling from Real Hibachi 👋 Saw your call - sorry we didn't catch you. Planning a hibachi party? Text me the date, headcount and city and I'll send your price right here.
```

**61+ 大单**
```
Hi! Rowling from Real Hibachi. [70-80] guests on [Dec 5] - love it, that's a [3]-chef party. It's $59.90 per adult (kids 5-12 $29.90, under 5 free); I'll put an exact number together tonight. Quick one: evening event, and roughly how many kids?
```

**邮件首条**（比短信长一点，仍然一屏）

## 在聊

- **问场合**：`What's the celebration?` / `Who's the party for?`
- **报城市后给价 + 链接 + 问时间**：`Tustin is inside our free 50 miles, so it's $1,018.20 for 18 on Saturday, Nov 7, and the headcount can change up to the day before. Here's the link to lock the date - it just saves a card, nothing is charged today: <link> What time are you thinking?`
- **人数在涨**：`Congrats on the bigger crowd! Hibachi actually scales well - the group discount grows with your headcount, parties of 20+ get a free appetizer, and past 28 we send a second chef so everyone eats at once. Roughly how many are you expecting now?`
- **改了时间地点、马上要锁**：`Yes to both! [Saturday, Oct 24 at 3:30 PM at your house in Granada Hills] works, and it's still $[988.50]. Here's the link with [3:30] and [Granada Hills] filled in: <link>`
- **订前问桌椅**（周六）：`They're normally $10 a guest - lock the date and I'll do them at half price, $5 a guest.`（周日到周五：`We do - they're normally $10 a guest, but lock the date today and I'll include them.`；没日期：`Pick your date and lock it, and I'll take care of the tables.`）
- **模板 L · 他以为要等人数定了才能订**（10-01）：`You don't need the final number to lock [Nov 7] - the link holds it (it just saves a card, nothing is charged today), and the headcount can change later: <link>` 发完挂 3–5 天，之后不追。
- **确认类，一句话**：`Done - 7:30 it is.`
- **客气收尾**：`Of course - enjoy your night as well. Talk soon.` / `Anytime - that's what this line is for.` / `Perfect - talk soon.`
- **他说要电话**：`Got it - we'll give you a call from 213-770-7788 shortly.`
- **他问是不是真人**：见 `answers.md` 末尾和 SKILL §2。

## 跟进（沉默的人，3 条封顶，SKILL §5.3）

**f_one**：台阶 + 一个真理由 + 一个好答的问题，≤200 字符，不带链接、不重复价格。
```
Hi, it's Rowling - figured you've got a lot going on. Has [Nov 7] settled, or is it still moving?          ← 有日期
Hi, it's Rowling - figured the group's still comparing calendars. Did a weekend win yet?                  ← 没日期
Hi, it's Rowling - one thing I didn't mention: with [20] guests you also get a free appetizer, your pick of gyoza, edamame or spring rolls. Is [Saturday, Nov 14] still the plan?   ← 有新的真事实
Hi, it's Rowling - [April 24] is a ways off, so no rush. Whenever you're ready, the link holds the date with just a card. Want me to check back in the new year?   ← 远期
```
不用 "Last note from me" / "last check-in so I don't crowd your texts"。

**B 桶 · 他定过回话时间**（到点接着他的话）：`Hi [Yvonne], it's Rowling - did your daughter give Saturday, Nov 7 the thumbs up?`

**f_deadline · 派对 7 天内、有新信息**（一条为限）：`Hi! Rowling here - Saturday's coming up fast. If you lock it in by Thursday, we shop fresh on Friday and you're all set. What city is the party in?`

## 不订、选了别家、预算不合

- **问原因**（SKILL §0）：`Totally understand - if you don't mind me asking, what tipped it? It helps us get better.`
- **祝福收尾**：`That sounds like a great plan - I hope it's a party everyone remembers! 🎉`
- 原因是别家更便宜：`negotiation.md` §7 挽留一次。

## 锁单之后

- **派对页 + 二选一**：`Here's your party page for everyone to pick their proteins: <link> Or just text me the list and I'll fill it in for you - whichever is easier.`
- **锁单后接一句**：`You're all set for [Saturday at 3:30] - thank you! [Shrimp is already on everyone's plate as the third protein, so] just add each guest's 2 on the party page, or text me the list and I'll fill it in.`
- **问地址**：`Could you send me the street address when you have a minute?`
- **问小孩年龄**：见 `answers.md` §吃什么。
- **免费小孩确认**：`Quick check for tomorrow: are [three] of the kids 3-4 (those eat free), or are all [8] between 5 and 12?`
- **师傅名字（前一天）**：`Your chef is [Blu] - he'll be there around [5] to set up. I'm still your contact, text me anytime.`
- **发票**（`desk order send` 自带）：`Here's your invoice for [Friday]: <link> - please look it over and reply "confirm" here if it's all right. A PDF copy is in your email too.`
- **问来源**（只问系统归不了因的、锁单后一次，搭在客气话后）：`Got it, thank you! Just curious - how did you find us?`

## 西语（客人写西语才用，数字口径一样）

```
B: ¡Hola! Soy Rowling de Real Hibachi 👋 [El sábado 18 de octubre] está disponible para tus [N] en [Ciudad] - ¿qué celebran?
precio + link: [Tustin] está dentro de nuestras 50 millas gratis: son $[1,018.20] para [18] el [sábado 7 de noviembre], y el número de invitados puede cambiar hasta el día anterior. Aquí puedes asegurar la fecha - solo se guarda una tarjeta, hoy no se cobra nada: <link>
f_one: Hola, soy Rowling - me imagino que andas con muchas cosas. ¿Ya quedó firme el [7 de noviembre] o todavía se está moviendo?
cierre: ¡Claro! Que disfruten mucho - aquí estoy para lo que necesites.
```
